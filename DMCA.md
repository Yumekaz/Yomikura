# Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Policy

Yomikura is a free and open-source static frontend web client. Yomikura does not host, mirror, stream, or distribute any copyrighted manga, comics, or media files, nor does it host or distribute third-party extension scraping code. All content retrieval and source logic is handled strictly by the user's self-hosted backend.

However, we respect intellectual property rights and are committed to complying with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent thereof and believe that any content in this repository (such as documentation, screenshots, or mock placeholders) infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a takedown notification.

## How to Submit a Takedown Notice

To be effective under the DMCA, your notice must be a written communication sent to the repository maintainers (either by opening a GitHub Issue or using the contact methods listed below) and include the following:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf;
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed;
3. Identification of the material in this repository that is claimed to be infringing, along with information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material (e.g., specific file paths, URLs, or line numbers);
4. Information reasonably sufficient to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and email address;
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and
6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

## Contact Information

Please submit takedown requests or legal inquiries by:
- Opening a **GitHub Issue** in this repository.
- Reaching out directly to the repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles.

*Note: Yomikura is a client-side frontend web shell. We cannot remove content from third-party websites, external databases, or user-controlled Suwayomi servers. If your claim relates to content retrieved by a user's server, you must contact the respective server host or content provider.*
